Places a spot type preview in paper inches. It shows one leader that runs down to the element point and one that runs up. X grows along the shoulder and canvas Y grows downward.

Remarks

The label follows Align's placement rule, SpotElevationTextAligner.ComputeTextOffset, with the margin LabelGapFromLeaderEnd and the gap LabelGapFromShoulder at a view factor of 1. Above Leader and Below Leader draw alike. The label sits on the point's side of the shoulder. In-line text sits past the leader end and has no symbol. The family drawing uses X along the shoulder and Y up. Its position mirrors vertically for the up spot; its text stays upright.
