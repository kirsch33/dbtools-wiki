Remarks

After a lookup of a binding, a rollback that undoes a change to its variance between group instances leaves a dead key in the binding map, unless the rolled-back changes inserted or reinserted the binding. Every later Contains or get_Item in that document then throws Autodesk.Revit.Exceptions.InvalidObjectException until the model is reopened. Another add-in's rollback, or an Undo of that setting, can leave this state. These lookups report it as a SafeExecutionRefusedException with InvalidStateMessage.
