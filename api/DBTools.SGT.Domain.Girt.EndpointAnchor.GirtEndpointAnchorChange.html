DBTools.SGT.Domain.Girt.EndpointAnchor

One girt endpoint the resolver re-anchored. CauseKey is the lost opening key or grid name. For a row that survives, Next is the endpoint's anchor after sibling merges. For a row merged into a sibling, Next is the anchor it was re-anchored to before the merge, and MergedMemberKeys maps its key to the surviving row's key.
