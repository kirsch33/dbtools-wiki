Loads raw markup after copying it out of collectible manifest resource streams. Stable CLR and URI namespaces resolve to the exact Presentation and HandyControl assemblies used by this host; the per-parse schema rejects payload and other non-framework assembly references so deferred templates cannot retain another generation. Resource URIs use the version-stamping ResourceUriExtension or HandyControlResourceUriExtension.
