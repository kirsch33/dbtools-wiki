Girt

Horizontal girt: runs along-wall, width spans vertical, depth points exterior. Flanges face up.

Head

Horizontal opening member (head): same as girt directionality and flange orientation. Away-from-opening seating rules apply. Flanges face up.

JambLeft

Visual-left jamb role from the exterior view. This is the higher-T jamb edge in the wall frame.

JambRight

Visual-right jamb role from the exterior view. This is the lower-T jamb edge in the wall frame.

Sill

Horizontal opening member (sill): runs along-wall with flanges down. Away-from-opening seating rules apply. Flanges face down.
