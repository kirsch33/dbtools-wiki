Namespace DBTools.SGT.Domain.Girt.SegmentBoundary

Classes

GirtSegmentBoundary

One place a girt may start or end: its anchor and its normalized position along the wall.

GirtSegmentBoundarySet

Defines where one girt, at one elevation, may start, end, and split. A blocking opening spans the elevation (inclusive), is framed, is not bypassed by the row, is not the row's interior opening, and overlaps the wall. Start-capable boundaries are the wall start and each blocking opening's higher edge; end-capable boundaries are each blocking opening's lower edge and the wall end. Split(GirtSegmentBoundary, GirtSegmentBoundary) is the only girt segment computation: plan-row segmentation, preview, placement and the usable-span check all call it.

GirtSegmentBoundarySource

The wall, openings, grids and framing decision every girt boundary set is built from. FromOpenings(double, IReadOnlyList<OpeningDefinition>, IReadOnlyDictionary<string, double>) holds the only production framing predicate.

GirtSegmentBoundarySpan

One solid girt span between a start-capable and an end-capable boundary.
