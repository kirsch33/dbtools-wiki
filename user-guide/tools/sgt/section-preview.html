SGT section preview

The section preview in the Super Girt Tool shows a cross-section of the wall at the position of the section marker on the elevation preview.

Section marker

The section marker is the blue dashed vertical line on the elevation preview. Drag it to change the cut position. The marker snaps to 6-inch steps along the wall length. The filled triangles at its top and bottom point screen-left, the viewing direction.

Dragging the marker changes the view only. Member end points keep snapping to valid targets: elements, grids and wall edges.

Viewing direction

The section looks from the exterior side of the wall toward the interior. Screen-left is the exterior face, and screen-right is the interior face.

Horizontal members (girts, headers, sills) are drawn from their resolved section profiles. When the cut meets a jamb edge, the jamb is drawn as a vertical depth band over its placed height, which matches the Revit output.

Compound wall layers

For a compound wall, the section draws each layer as a band across the wall thickness. Each band is labeled Material - thickness. The layer selected as the depth plane is highlighted, so you can see which layer the placement targets.

Far clip

The section shows only elements whose extent along the wall meets the clip range at the cut. The range works like the far clip offset of a Revit section view.

  • The range is 2'-0" long, from the section marker to 2'-0" on its screen-left side.
  • The elevation preview shows the range as a translucent blue band, bounded on the left by a dashed line. No distance label is shown.
  • Girts, headers, sills and jambs that lie fully outside the range are hidden in the section.
